The Story

There was once a man who was trusted by the people around him.

Then money entered the equation.

He chose greed over loyalty and betrayed the people who had placed their trust in him.

"You can sell someone's trust for money... but you can't buy it back."

And somehow, things got considerably worse from there.

The Three Stages of Betrayal

Stage 1 — Trust

Everyone believed he could be trusted. He was welcomed and given responsibility.

Stage 2 — Greed

Then he discovered that betrayal could make him money. He chose the money.

Stage 3 — Consequences

Eventually he discovered the oldest lesson of all: actions have consequences.

The Lesson

This story isn't about an entire group of people.

It's about one fictional person who made the choice to betray people who trusted him.

"Never betray the people who trust you."

Money disappears. Reputation can disappear. Trust is much harder to rebuild.
