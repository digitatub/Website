An unnecessarily serious investigation into an extremely important sentence.

What Does It Mean?

At first glance, the phrase appears simple. But beneath those seven words lies a complicated emotional, cultural, and possibly culinary journey.

“You met me at a very Chinese time in my life.”

Was it the food? The culture? The vibes? The sudden appearance of an unreasonable amount of fried rice? Nobody truly knows.

The Three Stages

Stage 1 — Discovery

Something happens. Nobody knows exactly what. But suddenly, everything feels slightly more Chinese.

Stage 2 — Acceptance

At this point, resistance is pointless. The Chinese era has officially begun.

Stage 3 — Enlightenment

You realize that the phrase was never really about China. It was about the journey.

The Science

Researchers have spent absolutely no time studying this phenomenon. Nevertheless, preliminary evidence suggests that the phrase becomes approximately 73% funnier every time it is repeated.

Further research is required.

And That's the Story.

You didn't meet me at a normal time in my life.

You met me at a VERY Chinese time in my life.
